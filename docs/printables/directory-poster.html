Public Call Office
How to Make a Telephone Call
  1. Lift the receiver and listen for the dialling tone (a continuous purring note).
  2. Insert twopence (2d.) in the coin slot at the top of the box.
  3. Dial the number required, turning the dial fully to the finger-stop for each figure and removing the finger to allow the dial to return freely.
  4. When the called person answers, press Button A firmly. The coins will drop and you may speak.
  5. If there is no reply, or if the line is engaged, press Button B to recover your coins from the cup below.
  6. When the conversation is finished, replace the receiver on its rest.
BUTTON A

Press when the called person answers.
Your coins will be collected and you may speak.

BUTTON B

Press if there is no reply or the line is engaged.
Your coins will be returned.

Emergency Calls

For Fire, Police or Ambulance

Dial 9 9 9

No coins are required for emergency calls.

Lift the receiver, dial 999, and state which service you require.

Important Notices

Callers requiring trunk calls should dial 0 for the Operator, who will connect the call and advise the charge. Insert additional coins as directed before pressing Button A.

The minimum charge for a trunk call from this call office is 4d. Insert the required fee before the Operator connects the call.

Should the telephone be found out of order, kindly report the fault to the nearest Post Office or dial 0 for the Operator.

Wilful damage to Post Office property is punishable by law.
