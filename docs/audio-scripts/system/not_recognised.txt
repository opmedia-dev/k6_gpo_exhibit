NOT RECOGNISED — Played when an unknown number is dialled
=========================================================
File: /system/not_recognised.mp3
Estimated duration: ~12 seconds

--- SCRIPT ---

[In a formal, clipped GPO intercept voice]

"The number you have dialled has not been recognised. Please replace the handset and try again."

[Brief pause]

"You may dial one-zero-zero for operator assistance."

--- NOTES ---
- Formal, precise, slightly stern — the classic GPO intercept tone
- This plays every time someone dials a number not in the directory
- Keep it short — visitors will hear this often
- The "dial 100" hint encourages them to try another number
- Consider adding a single "pip" tone before the voice starts
