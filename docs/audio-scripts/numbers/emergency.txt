EMERGENCY — Dial 999
====================
File: /numbers/emergency.mp3
Estimated duration: ~35 seconds

--- SCRIPT ---

[In a calm, professional voice]

"Emergency. Which service do you require — police, fire, or ambulance?"

[Brief pause]

"Connecting you now. Please stay on the line."

[Brief pause, then shift to informative narrator voice]

"Nine-nine-nine is the United Kingdom's emergency telephone number, and the oldest in the world. It was introduced in nineteen thirty-seven after a devastating fire in London highlighted the need for a dedicated emergency line. Today, it handles over thirty million calls a year. This has been a recorded demonstration — in a real emergency, always dial nine-nine-nine."
