SPEAKING CLOCK — Dial 123
=========================
File: /numbers/speaking_clock.mp3
Estimated duration: ~25 seconds

--- SCRIPT ---

[In a precise, formal voice — imitating the classic speaking clock cadence]

"At the third stroke, the time sponsored by Accurist will be twelve o'clock precisely."

[Three short pips]

"At the third stroke, the time will be twelve o'clock and ten seconds."

[Three short pips]

[Brief pause, then narrator voice]

"The Speaking Clock, known affectionately as TIM, has been telling Britain the time since nineteen thirty-six. At its peak, it received over two hundred and fifty million calls a year."

--- NOTES ---
- The speaking clock voice should be precise, measured, and distinctly formal
- The three pips are essential — short, evenly spaced beeps
- Use a fixed time (twelve o'clock) rather than trying to be accurate
- The pips can be generated as 1kHz tone, 100ms each, 500ms apart
