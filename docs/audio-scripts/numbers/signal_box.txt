SIGNAL BOX — Dial 501
=====================
File: /numbers/signal_box.mp3
Estimated duration: ~30 seconds

--- SCRIPT ---

[Sound of lever being pulled, a mechanical clunk]

[In a gruff, busy voice — a working signalman]

"Signal box. Yes?"

[Brief pause]

"Right, the down express has just cleared the section. I'm pulling off the signals for your goods train now. You're clear through to the junction."

[Sound of another lever, bell ringing twice]

"That's two bells — train entering section. Keep your eyes peeled at the crossing, the gates are set."

[Brief pause]

"I've got to go, there's another one coming up behind. Cheerio."

--- NOTES ---
- Busy, no-nonsense working man's voice
- The signalman is in the middle of something — the caller is interrupting
- Bell codes are authentic: 2 bells = "train entering section"
- Sound effects: lever pulls (mechanical clunks), block bell rings
- This gives visitors a feel for the real working railway
