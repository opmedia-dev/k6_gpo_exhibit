--- SCRIPT ---

[In a helpful, patient voice — someone used to answering the same questions]

"Railway timetable enquiries. Where are you travelling from and to?"

[Brief pause]

"Right, let me have a look for you. There's a service at nine forty-five, arriving at eleven twenty. You'll need to change at Crewe — that's a twelve-minute connection on platform four."

[Brief pause]

"Or if you'd prefer a direct train, there's one at ten thirty, but that's the slow service and doesn't arrive until quarter past twelve."

[Brief pause]

"Shall I book you a seat? No? Very well. Have a pleasant journey."
