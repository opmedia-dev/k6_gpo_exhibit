SPOOKY — Dial 666
=================
File: /numbers/spooky.mp3
Estimated duration: ~20 seconds

--- SCRIPT ---

[Heavy static and crackling on the line]

[A distant, echoey voice — barely audible through interference]

"Hello...? Can you hear me...?"

[More static]

"I've been trying to get through for such a long time..."

[The crackling intensifies, then a long pause]

"The last train left... a very long time ago..."

[Static fades to silence]

[Three slow, deep bell tolls]

--- NOTES ---
- Heavy audio processing: reverb, static, low-pass filter
- The voice should sound distant, as if calling from far away
- The "last train" reference ties it to the railway setting
- The bell tolls are a classic gothic touch
- Keep it atmospheric rather than scary — suitable for all ages
- This should feel like a ghost story, not a horror film
