PARCELS OFFICE — Dial 742
=========================
File: /numbers/parcels.mp3
Estimated duration: ~25 seconds

--- SCRIPT ---

[In a cheerful, busy voice — someone working behind a counter]

"Railway parcels office, good afternoon."

[Brief pause]

"Yes, we can send that for you. If you bring it to the parcels counter before three o'clock, it'll go on the four fifteen to Manchester and arrive first thing tomorrow morning."

[Brief pause]

"The charge depends on the weight. Up to seven pounds it's one and sixpence, over that it's two and nine. Cash on collection is available for an extra threepence."

[Brief pause]

"Right you are. We'll see you before three then. Goodbye."
